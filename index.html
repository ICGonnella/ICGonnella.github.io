About & Research

Hi, I’m Isabella! I’m a final-year PhD candidate in Mathematical Analysis, Modelling and Applications at SISSA in Trieste, supervised by Prof. Gianluigi Rozza and Dr. Federico Pichi at SISSA, together with Prof. Olga Mula at the University of Vienna.

My research lies at the intersection of numerical analysis and machine learning, where I explore how mathematical structure can guide the design of efficient computational methods. My work focuses on reduced-order approximations of Wasserstein gradient flows and stochastic approaches to nonlinear bifurcation problems. I’m also interested in the mathematical foundations of learning algorithms, particularly in modelling their dynamics and characterising their convergence properties.
